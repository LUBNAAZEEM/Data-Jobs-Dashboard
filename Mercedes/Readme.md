## Executive Sales Overview
Mercedes-Benz experienced revenue growth peaks in **2021** and **2023**, followed by a slowdown post-2023, indicating possible market saturation or pricing pressure.

- **Total Sales Volume:** 12M  
- **Average Base Price:** $104K  
- **Total Revenue:** $1T  


![SALES OVERVIEW](/Mercedes/Images/Sales%20Performance.png)


- Turbocharged vehicles dominate sales (**85% of total sales**)  
- Indicates strong customer preference for **performance-enhancing features**

- Most popular colors: **Black, White, Silver**  
- Suggests **conservative buying behavior** and focus on **resale value**

- Average vehicle price shows a **steady increase from 2020 to 2025**  
- Reflects **consistent pricing strategy** and potential **feature upgrades** over time

- **GLC** and **GLE** lead in revenue and volume  
- Sales gradually decrease across smaller and AMG models  
- Highlights **strong preference for mid-to-large SUV models**

## Impact of Vehicle Performance and Design on Sales

![VEHICLE PERFROMANCE AND DESIGN](/Mercedes/Images/vehicle%20performance%20and%20design.png)

### Horsepower vs Sales Volume by Model
- This scatter plot analyzes the relationship between vehicle performance and demand.
- Higher horsepower generally correlates with higher sales.
- Several **mid-horsepower models outperform high-performance models**, indicating that practicality and pricing also drive demand.
- **EVs** cluster differently, while **Petrol** dominates high-horsepower models.

**Total Sales by Fuel Type:**
- Petrol (purple) dominates high horsepower.  
- Hybrid/Electric cluster in mid–low horsepower.  
- Diesel mostly sits mid-range.  

**Insight:**  
Petrol vehicles dominate total sales, while Hybrid and Electric vehicles show lower volumes but represent **growth opportunities**.  

**Trend Observation:**  
Higher horsepower does **not** guarantee higher sales volume — a non-obvious insight that provides a strategic edge.

**Conclusion:**  
Mid-horsepower vehicles, especially petrol and hybrid models, contribute most to total sales. Customers prioritize a balance between **performance, efficiency, and price**.

> “Petrol vehicles still dominate overall sales volume, but hybrid and electric models show a growing contribution, highlighting Mercedes-Benz’s transition toward electrification while maintaining strong ICE demand.”

## Customer Preferences & Product Strategy

![CUSTOMER PREFERENCE](/Mercedes/Images/Customer%20preference.png)
### Customer Preference Analysis
- **Turbocharged Vehicles:** Account for over **85% of sales**, confirming strong demand for performance-enhancing features.  
- **Top Vehicle Colors:** Neutral colors (Black, White, Silver) dominate, indicating conservative buying behavior.  
- **Sales Distribution by Fuel Type:** Petrol remains dominant; Hybrid and Electric vehicles together contribute significantly, supporting Mercedes’ electrification strategy.

### Key Findings
1. Revenue is driven more by **mid-range performance models** than high-horsepower vehicles.  
2. **Turbocharged models** command strong customer preference.  
3. Petrol vehicles dominate current sales, but **EVs represent future growth**.  
4. Neutral color preferences indicate **brand consistency and resale value focus**.  
5. Post-2023 sales decline suggests **market maturity or pricing challenges**.

---

### Conclusion
Balanced performance vehicles and feature-rich models drive sales today, while **electrification represents Mercedes-Benz’s strongest long-term opportunity**.
